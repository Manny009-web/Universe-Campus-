🛡️ Privacy Policy & Terms

Support and advertising chats

Messages you send to UNIVERSE Support are stored securely in the app and are visible only to you and the admin team. If you tick the box, your phone model and app version are added to help fix problems. The Advertise page opens WhatsApp, which handles those messages under its own terms. Notifications are optional and you can turn them off in your phone settings.

Your data rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Open Settings to download a copy of your data or to ask support to delete your account.

Background alerts

If you turn on notifications, we save your phone\u2019s push address, your role, faculty, level and department so we can alert you about things posted for you. It does not contain your name. Turn notifications off in your phone settings to stop them.

AI assistant

Messages you send to UNIVERSE AI are processed by our AI provider to write a reply. Do not share passwords, bank details or other sensitive information. Your chat history is saved only on your phone and you can clear it any time.

Cookies, storage and analytics

UNIVERSE only uses essential browser storage (your login, theme, tour and notice settings). Optional, privacy-friendly visit statistics are off unless you accept them. You can change your choice any time.
